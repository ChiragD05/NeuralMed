Pneumonia is a lung infection that may cause fever, cough, chest pain, and difficulty breathing. Treatment may include antibiotics, rest, hydration, and oxygen support in severe cases.

COVID-19 symptoms may include fever, cough, fatigue, sore throat, breathing difficulty, and loss of smell or taste. Severe cases require medical evaluation.

Asthma is a chronic respiratory disease causing wheezing, chest tightness, shortness of breath, and coughing. Inhalers are commonly used for treatment.

Diabetes is a chronic disease affecting blood sugar regulation. Symptoms may include increased thirst, frequent urination, fatigue, and blurred vision.

Hypertension, also known as high blood pressure, increases the risk of heart disease and stroke. Lifestyle modification and medication may be required.

Tuberculosis is a bacterial infection that primarily affects the lungs and may cause persistent cough, fever, night sweats, and weight loss.

Heart disease may cause chest pain, shortness of breath, dizziness, fatigue, and irregular heartbeat.

Stroke symptoms may include facial drooping, weakness on one side of the body, slurred speech, and confusion. Immediate medical attention is critical.

Anemia is a condition involving low red blood cell count and may cause fatigue, weakness, dizziness, and pale skin.

Migraine is a neurological condition that may cause severe headaches, nausea, vomiting, and sensitivity to light or sound.
